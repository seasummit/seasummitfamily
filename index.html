Questions people actually ask

Isn't the Google listing free?
Yes. A Google Business Profile is a free Google product. What you pay me for is the work — never for the listing itself.
Will you get me to the top of Google?
No, and be careful with anyone who says they will. Google doesn't promise to show any website in its results. What I promise is the work: a complete, accurate listing, a site that loads, and a review system that asks everybody.
Who owns it all afterwards?
You do. The listing stays in your name with you as owner. I'm added as a manager and you can remove me in two clicks. You keep your own copy of the site files.
Can you write me some reviews?
No. Fake reviews violate Google's policies and federal law. The system I set up asks your real customers, all of them, the same way.
What if I want to stop?
The monthly is month-to-month. Stop it whenever you like and you keep the site, the listing and every review you earned.
