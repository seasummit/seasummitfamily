Questions people actually ask

Will callers know they're talking to AI?

Yes. Every call starts with the assistant saying it's an AI assistant. Anyone who wants a person can ask and be transferred to you, or leave a message that's texted to you right away.

Are calls recorded?

Only if you want them to be. Recordings let you hear what the assistant said and fix anything it got wrong. If calls are recorded, callers are told at the start, because Florida requires everyone on a call to agree to recording.

What does the AI tool cost, and who pays it?

You pay the tool's maker directly, usually from about $25 a month for a small business. It's in your name, I don't mark it up, and it keeps working if you stop working with me.

Isn't the Google listing free?

Yes. A Google Business Profile is a free Google product. What you pay me for is the work, never the listing itself.

Will you get me to the top of Google?

No, and be careful with anyone who says they will. Google doesn't promise to show any website in its results. What I promise is the work: a complete, accurate listing, a site that loads, and a review system that asks everybody.

I don't have any reviews yet. Is that a problem?

No. That's where every business starts. The review system is how the first ones come in.

Who owns it all afterwards?

You do. The listing stays in your name with you as owner. I'm added as a manager and you can remove me in two clicks. You keep your own copy of the site files.

Can you write me some reviews?

No. Fake reviews violate Google's policies and federal law. The system I set up asks your real customers, all of them, the same way.

What if I want to stop?

The monthly is month-to-month. Stop it whenever you like and you keep the site, the listing and every review you earned.
